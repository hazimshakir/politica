The framework

Seven dimensions. More than one label.

Political beliefs rarely fit on a single left-to-right line, which is why a one-line political spectrum can feel limiting. POLITICA looks at seven dimensions independently and treats mixed or uncertain views as meaningful answers, not mistakes.

Four enduring ideas

Ideas that every tradition argues about.

Democracy, liberty, equality and justice are claimed, defined and balanced differently across political traditions and countries. POLITICA asks how you weigh them, and does not tell you which weighting is right.

Democracy

How power is shared, challenged and held to account.

Liberty

How freedom is protected, and where it may be limited.

Equality

How opportunity, status and outcomes are treated.

Justice

How fairness is defined and applied.

How it works

Thirty-five questions. A map of your thinking.

1

Answer

Respond to 35 statements on a five-point scale, from strongly disagree to strongly agree. Some come with short scenarios.

2

Explore

See where you sit on each of the seven dimensions, with plain-language explanations of what your answers suggest.

3

Reflect

Look at the mixed or competing views in your answers. They are often the most interesting part.

What POLITICA is

  • An educational tool for self-reflection
  • Independent and non-partisan
  • Transparent about how results are produced
  • Your answers are saved only in your own browser and never sent to a server

What POLITICA is not

  • Not a scientifically validated test
  • Not advice on parties, candidates or voting
  • Not a label that says who you “really” are
  • Not a judgement on any political view
