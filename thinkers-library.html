About this pilot collection

Profiles and dimension mappings are AI-assisted and provisional, not independently validated scholarly assessments. Sources are listed so readers can examine the underlying texts. A cited source does not, by itself, prove that an interpretation or score is correct. Historical figures may have changed their views over time; where possible, distinct periods are shown. Blank or unscored dimensions mean the evidence is insufficient or the modern category does not fit.
